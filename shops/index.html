Independent shops are where so many great collections begin. I’m building Thirty Three, a new iPhone companion for vinyl collectors, and I’d love your perspective on how it could help people discover more music — and more independent shops.

Thirty Three lets collectors connect their Discogs collection, choose what to play and explore records they might want next. The aim is to complement the experience of visiting a great record shop, not replace it.
