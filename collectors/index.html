Hello,

There’s nothing quite like finding a record you love. Thirty Three is a new iPhone companion for vinyl collectors, built to make exploring your shelves, deciding what to play and discovering what’s next a pleasure.

Connect your Discogs account to bring your collection into Thirty Three. Explore familiar favourites in a fresh way, keep track of what you play, and get more from the records you already own.
